# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-27)

First release.

- Capture toolbar with screen, window and region modes, plus screen and region recording.
- Frozen-frame selection with crosshair guides, resize handles, size readout and arrow-key
  nudging, plus an optional pixel magnifier.
- Window picking on Hyprland and sway; Space switches between region and window selection.
- Capture flash and shutter sound.
- Floating thumbnail with copy, mark up, show in folder, delete and drag and drop.
- Screen recording through wl-screenrec (GPU) or wf-recorder, with microphone and system
  audio, Standard and High Quality, and H.264, HEVC, VP9 or AV1.
- Recordings are made share-friendly after stopping (MP4 index at the front, correct H.264 level).
- `status.json` and `nice-screenshots stop` for status bar stop buttons, with a Quickshell example.
